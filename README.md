# ✦ About Me

I'm **Shreya Mukherjee**, a **Bachelor's graduate in Finance** building my career at the intersection of **Finance, Data, Business, and Technology**.

My journey into analytics comes from a simple idea: **numbers become more valuable when we understand what they mean.**

My Finance background taught me to think about performance, value, risk, profitability, and business decisions. I'm now building on that foundation through hands-on work in **Data Analytics, Business Analytics, Financial Analytics, Business Intelligence, and Data Visualization**.

I enjoy taking a business question, exploring the data behind it, finding patterns, and turning those findings into something that can support a better decision.

I'm currently developing practical skills across **SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI, Tableau, Pandas, NumPy, Streamlit, and Plotly**, while also exploring **AI / GenAI and AI-assisted analytics**.

> **I don't want to analyse data just to describe what happened.  
> I want to understand why it happened — and what could be done next.**

# ✦ A Little More About My Approach

I believe good analytics sits somewhere between **numbers and judgement**.

A dashboard can tell us *what happened*.

Analysis should help us understand *why it happened*.

Business thinking should help us ask *what happens next*.

That's the direction I'm trying to build toward.

> **From numbers → to patterns → to insights → to decisions.**
